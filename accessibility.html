Our commitment

HERO Foundation serves veterans, first responders, nurses, teachers, EMTs, police officers, and firefighters, including people living with injuries, post-traumatic stress, and other disabilities. We want everyone to be able to read about our work, find out how to take part, and donate without barriers. Accessibility is part of the mission, not an add-on.

The standard we work to

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. We review the site against those guidelines when we make changes and we fix what we find.

What we have done

  • Pages use a logical heading structure and landmarks so screen-reader users can move through them quickly.
  • Every meaningful image has descriptive alternative text. Decorative images and background video are hidden from assistive technology.
  • All links and buttons can be reached and operated with a keyboard, and keyboard focus is visible.
  • Text keeps strong contrast against the background, including where it sits over photographs and video.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops, and text can be enlarged to 200 percent without loss of content.
  • A "Skip to content" link is the first item on every page for keyboard users.
  • Nothing flashes, and nothing is timed or expires.

Motion and video

The home page uses short, silent, looping video clips and a scrolling diver animation as decoration. They carry no information that is not also in the text. If you have turned on "Reduce motion" in your operating system or browser settings, the videos are replaced by still images and the diver does not move.

Known limitations

We are aware of these limitations and are working on them:

  • The program carousel on the home page is a visual, rotating element. Its content is available to screen readers as ordinary text, and the same information appears in the FAQ, but the rotating presentation may be harder to follow for some users.
  • Donations are completed on a checkout page operated by Clover, and our social-media profiles are on Instagram and LinkedIn. We do not control the accessibility of those services.

Browsers and assistive technology

The site is built with standard HTML and CSS and is designed to work with current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge, and with screen readers such as VoiceOver, NVDA, and JAWS. It does not require JavaScript to read any content.

Tell us about a barrier

If any part of this website is difficult for you to use, please tell us. Email info@herofound.org with the page address, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you were using. We aim to respond within five business days, and we will provide the information you need in another format if we cannot fix the problem right away.
